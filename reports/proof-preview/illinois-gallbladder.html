When this costs more

You're sedated

Anesthesia is its own company

Anesthesia is usually billed by a separate group, by the minute. At an in-network hospital or surgery center, the No Surprises Act stops an out-of-network anesthesiologist from billing you more than your in-network share.

Something more is done

Another code, another price

If the doctor does more than planned, it can be billed under an added or different code. Ask what codes they expect to bill before you schedule.

Questions people ask

Gallbladder removal cost: the short answers

How much does a gallbladder removal cost without insurance in Illinois?

The lowest cash price we found in Illinois is $352.80 at Fairfield Memorial Hospital 1, from the hospital's own price file; that covers the hospital's fee only, and the doctor and anesthesia bill separately. We found 40 cash prices in all. If you pay cash, ask for a written Good Faith Estimate when you schedule; you can dispute a bill that comes in $400 or more above it.

How much does a gallbladder removal cost with insurance in Illinois?

It depends on your insurer's network and where you go. Published negotiated facility fees in Illinois run from $995.00 to $33,601.00. That rate is the total; what you owe depends on your plan. Before you meet your deductible you may pay the whole negotiated rate; after that, your coinsurance or copay.

Why do gallbladder removal prices vary so much?

The highest published rate is 34 times the lowest. Each insurer negotiates its own contract with each hospital, surgery center and doctor, so the same service has many prices. Here, the lowest hospital rate ($998.60) is above the lowest surgery center rate ($995.00). Even at one place, Hshs Holy Family Hospital Inc, rates range 9.1-fold depending on the insurer.

What does the price of a gallbladder removal include?

The facility fee at the hospital or surgery center: the room, equipment, nursing staff and supplies. Not included: the doctor's fee, anesthesia and pathology (if tissue goes to a lab) bill separately, and so do follow-up visits. If more is done during the procedure, a different code can be billed.

What does Medicare pay for a gallbladder removal?

Traditional Medicare's 2026 rate is $6,176.47 (hospital outpatient facility fee, national rate before local adjustment, Chicago Medicare area). It's a benchmark; people with private insurance or no insurance can't get Medicare's rate.
