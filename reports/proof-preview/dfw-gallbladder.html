When this costs more

You're sedated

Anesthesia is its own company

Anesthesia is usually billed by a separate group, by the minute. At an in-network hospital or surgery center, the No Surprises Act stops an out-of-network anesthesiologist from billing you more than your in-network share.

Something more is done

Another code, another price

If the doctor does more than planned, it can be billed under an added or different code. Ask what codes they expect to bill before you schedule.

Read the fine print

What the price covers

Included

The facility fee at the hospital or surgery center: the room, equipment, nursing staff and supplies.

Not included

The doctor's fee, anesthesia and pathology (if tissue goes to a lab) bill separately, and so do follow-up visits. If more is done during the procedure, a different code can be billed.

What you'd pay

A negotiated rate is the total the insurer and provider agreed. Until you meet your deductible you may owe all of it; after that, your coinsurance or copay. The Plan Calculator does the math for DFW marketplace plans.

Questions people ask

Gallbladder removal cost: the short answers

How much does a gallbladder removal cost without insurance in Dallas–Fort Worth?

The lowest cash price we found in Dallas–Fort Worth is $418.00 at North Texas Medical Center, from the hospital's own price file; that covers the hospital's fee only, and the doctor and anesthesia bill separately. We found 12 cash prices in all. If you pay cash, ask for a written Good Faith Estimate when you schedule; you can dispute a bill that comes in $400 or more above it.

How much does a gallbladder removal cost with insurance in Dallas–Fort Worth?

It depends on your insurer's network and where you go. Published negotiated facility fees in Dallas–Fort Worth run from $1,755.00 to $30,736.00. That rate is the total; what you owe depends on your plan. Before you meet your deductible you may pay the whole negotiated rate; after that, your coinsurance or copay.

Why do gallbladder removal prices vary so much?

The highest published rate is 18 times the lowest. Each insurer negotiates its own contract with each hospital, surgery center and doctor, so the same service has many prices. Even at one place, Dallas Medical Center LLC, rates range 10.1-fold depending on the insurer.

What does the price of a gallbladder removal include?

The facility fee at the hospital or surgery center: the room, equipment, nursing staff and supplies. Not included: the doctor's fee, anesthesia and pathology (if tissue goes to a lab) bill separately, and so do follow-up visits. If more is done during the procedure, a different code can be billed.

What does Medicare pay for a gallbladder removal?

Traditional Medicare's 2026 rate is $6,176.47 (hospital outpatient facility fee, national rate before local adjustment, Dallas area). It's a benchmark; people with private insurance or no insurance can't get Medicare's rate.
